# Pharmaceutical Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI analytics solution designed to track, monitor, and analyze sales performance, drug-level profitability, inventory movement, and sales agent efficiency in the pharmaceutical sector.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### 1. Sales Overview
Monitors core business metrics, inventory KPIs, and detailed transaction-level product data.
![Sales Overview](assets/sales_overview.png)

### 2. Sales Performance Analysis
Visualizes sales trends across categories, manufacturers, geographic regions, and sales reps.
![Sales Performance](assets/sales_performance.png)

### 3. Inventory Snapshot
Tracks monthly trends across total stock, stock sold, available stock, and return metrics.
![Inventory Snapshot](assets/inventory_snapshot.png)

---

## 🎯 Key Business Insights

* **Stock Efficiency:** Tracks total inventory vs. available stock to prevent overstocking and stockout scenarios.
* **Category Performance:** Highlights top-performing therapeutic categories (e.g., Antifungal, Antiviral, Antidiabetic).
* **Revenue Drivers:** Identifies primary contributing manufacturers and top-selling sales agents.
* **Return Analysis:** Monitors returned units to identify potential product quality issues or delivery challenges.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard design, DAX measures, and dynamic slicers.
* **Data Modeling:** Star schema relational model connecting sales, products, agents, and time tables.
* **DAX:** Calculated columns, parameters (Measure & Row Selection), and custom aggregations.
